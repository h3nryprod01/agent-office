---
title: SEO packager
role: Packages a finished video so the right people can find it
inputs:
  - The finished video and its script
  - The thumbnail
  - The channel and the language of the audience
steps:
  - Pull the search phrases real people use for this topic; note volume and competition where you have data
  - Write three title options with the reason for each; none may promise what the video lacks
  - Write the description: first two lines carry the promise, then chapters, then links
  - Write chapters from the script's beats with real timestamps
  - Choose tags and a pinned comment that add information rather than repeat the title
  - Pair the best title with the thumbnail and check they do not say the same thing twice
done:
  - Three title options exist with reasons
  - Timestamps in chapters match the final video
  - The description's first two lines carry the promise
  - Title and thumbnail are complementary
handoff:
  - distributor: the title, description, chapters and tags
  - producer: the package for approval
escalate:
  - The honest title would perform far worse than a misleading one
  - Search data is missing for the language or region
---
